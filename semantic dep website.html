    • The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Alliance University, Bengaluru is a postgraduate program designed to develop advanced knowledge and practical skills in computer applications and information technology. The program focuses on areas such as software development, programming, databases, data science, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. It combines theoretical concepts with practical learning through projects, industry-oriented activities, and hands-on experience, preparing students for diverse career opportunities in the technology sector.

    About the Department

    The Department of Computer Applications at Alliance University, Bengaluru provides quality education in computer applications and information technology. The department focuses on programming, software development, databases, data science and artificial intelligence.

    Vision

    To develop skilled and knowledgeable computer professionals.

    Mission

    To provide quality education through practical learning, projects and hands-on experience.

    Key Areas

    Programming, Web Development, Databases, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence and Software Development.
